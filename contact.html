Quick answers

Frequently Asked Questions

How do I send a prescription?

Photograph the whole script in good light, including the prescriber's signature, and send it through the form above. We confirm by phone before dispensing anything.

Can I return medicines?

Unopened over-the-counter products and supplements can be returned within 30 days. Dispensed prescription medicines cannot be returned for safety reasons.

Do you deliver cold-chain items?

Yes. Insulin, vaccines and other temperature-sensitive medicines travel in insulated, monitored packaging and are never left in a hot vehicle.

Health tips from our pharmacists

One short email a month. Medicine reminders, seasonal advice, no spam.
