About this product

Vitamin D3 (cholecalciferol) is the form of vitamin D your skin makes from sunlight, and it is the form our pharmacists recommend for most adults during low-sunlight months. Each softgel delivers 1000 IU in a base of food-grade oil so it is absorbed alongside a normal meal.

  • Supports normal bone density, muscle function and immune response.
  • One softgel daily — simple, consistent dosing.
  • Free from artificial colours, gluten and added sugar.
  • Suitable for adults and children over 12 years.

Food supplements are not a substitute for a varied diet or prescribed medication. If you take prescription medicines, ask our pharmacist for a free interaction check before starting.
