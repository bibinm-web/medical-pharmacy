Our hospitality

Always open for your health service

VitaCure Care Pharmacy is an independent, family-run community pharmacy. We dispense prescriptions, run in-store health screenings and help people manage long-term conditions with a pharmacist who knows their name, their history and their repeat list.

We are pharmacist-led rather than sales-led: if a cheaper generic does the same job, we will tell you. If an over-the-counter product will not help, we will say so.

  • Registered pharmacy with a superintendent pharmacist on site.
  • Private consultation room for confidential advice.
  • Free medication reviews and interaction checks.
  • Cold-chain storage and monitored delivery for fragile medicines.

Our amazing story

The History

Twenty-eight years of standing behind the same counter — with a lot more equipment behind it now.

1998 — One corner counter

VitaCure opened as a single storefront with two staff, a hand-written prescription ledger and a promise to know every customer by name. That promise is still written on the wall behind our counter.

2011 — Clinical services

We added a private consultation room, blood pressure and glucose screening, and a pharmacist-led medication review service — turning a dispensing counter into a genuine primary-care partner.

Today — Delivered with care

Twelve thousand products, thirty-two licensed pharmacists and a cold-chain delivery network. Same mission, wider reach: your health, looked after properly.

How we work

Four Steps From Order To Doorstep

1. Send your script

Upload a photo, ask your doctor to send it, or bring the paper copy in.

2. Pharmacist review

A registered pharmacist checks dosage, interactions and alternatives.

3. Dispensed & packed

Labelled clearly, packed discreetly, cold-chain where required.

4. Delivered

Tracked delivery, or free collection from the counter, same day.

Accreditations

Standards We Hold Ourselves To

Every VitaCure pharmacist is registered with the state board and takes part in continuing professional development. Our premises, cold chain and record-keeping are inspected regularly.

  • GDP-compliant cold-chain handling and monitoring
  • Secure, encrypted handling of prescription records
  • Annual independent medication-safety audit
